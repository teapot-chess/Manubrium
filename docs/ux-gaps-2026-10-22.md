# UX gap audit, 2026-10-22

The same pass Alnasl had the day before. Every screen in `src/ui/` was read
for the lifecycle a person expects of what it shows: make, change, remove
(with a confirm that says what goes), undo where it is cheap; plus dead
ends, actions without feedback, errors shown as nothing or as raw text,
missing loading states, knowledge the app assumes and never gives, phone
layout, the accessibility basics, untranslated strings and stale help.
Code only: no browser, no phone. 1.0.0 is in App Store review, so nothing
here is urgent; the fixes ride along with the next build.

The headline: Android's back button had nothing to go back through. There
is no router and no history, so it put the app away - sheet, half-typed
incident and all - from any screen. (iPhone-only for now, so nobody has met
it yet. They would have.)

## Fixed in this pass

| Screen | Gap | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everywhere | Android's back button leaves the app from any screen or sheet; Escape does nothing in a browser | high (Android) | `src/back.ts`: back closes the sheet on top, then goes back a screen (tournament, new tournament, servers, add a server), and only at the tournament list puts the app away; Escape does the same |
| Every sheet | The focus stays on the page behind the sheet; a screen reader or keyboard starts outside the dialog and stays there after it closes | medium | The sheet takes the focus when it opens and hands it back to what opened it |
| More, Hall boards | The board code is shown once, and a tap beside the sheet threw it away for good | medium | That sheet closes only by its Done button (`Sheet sticky`) |
| Tournaments | Opened without a signal: an error, and no way to try again short of killing the app (Refresh only appears once a list has loaded) | medium | "Try again" under the error |
| A tournament | Same: an error and the back arrow, nothing else | medium | "Try again" |
| Players | A failed load had no retry; dismissing the error left a spinner that never ends | medium | "Try again" under the error |
| Players, a player's sheet | "Save the byes" closed the sheet, taking a name, rating or club typed above it, unsaved | medium | The sheet stays open, says "Byes saved.", and the list behind it is refreshed |
| Standings | A failed load had no retry (Refresh only appears once loaded) | low | "Try again" |
| A tournament | An archived or handed-off tournament: every board greyed out, no reason given | low | A notice says which, and that everything can still be read |
| More, Incidents and Hall boards | Nothing at all while loading; a failed incident load had no retry | low | Spinner while loading, "Try again" after a failure |
| More, Incidents | Kinds shown as the database spells them ("arbiter call", "game rewound") | low | The labels the incident dialog uses ("Arbiter call") |

| More, Settings | Raw tokens: `swiss`, `manual`, `1.0`, `BHC1, BH, SB` | low | Type, publishing and the bye score in words; tie-breaks by the names the server sends in `tiebreak_options` |
| More, Settings | Saving the mid-event settings closed the sheet and said nothing | low | "Settings saved." |
| Round, Pair and publish | The "Unpair round n?" question had only the button that does it; the way out was tapping beside the sheet | low | "Keep it" beside it |
| Round | An unrated player's line read " · 1.5" | low | "1.5" |
| Servers | No way back to the tournaments except picking the server already in use | low | A back arrow when a server is in use |
| Servers, Remove | "n results … not sent" counted incidents as results | low | "n results or incidents are on this phone not sent yet" |

Tests: `src/ui/screens.test.tsx` ("the way back", "a load that failed", "a
read-only tournament", "words, not tokens", "saving byes", "undoing a round").

## Checked and fine

Adding a server (scan or paste, the code's ten minutes and the desktop
copy's switch both explained, the help matches OpenPairings' Account page);
removing a server (asks, says what unsent entries are lost); the outbox
(nothing dropped quietly, refusals stay until dismissed, with the board
named); entering a result offline (shown as Waiting); confirming or
rejecting a Lite phone's result; the desk's questions on hand edits and
pairing (the server asks, the app shows it, nothing answered for the
arbiter); unpairing (asks, says a snapshot is kept); revoking a board code
(asks); incident photo (preview, remove before sending); check-in
(optimistic, put back on refusal); withdraw and re-enter (one tap each way);
new tournament (offline says so, Try again); share (says Copied / Could not
share). Layout: 16px inputs (no iOS zoom), safe-area insets on the top bar,
tab bar and sheets, long names ellipsised, standings table scrolls
sideways. Contrast: the soft text and warning colours pass 4.5:1 on their
backgrounds in both themes. Every input has a label; icon buttons have
`aria-label`s.

## Needs a decision

| Screen | Gap | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Everywhere | The app is English only. The arbiters it is for are mostly Dutch- and French-speaking; the OpenPairings desk and Lite are translated, this is not. | (a) NL (with "je") and FR in the next build, following the phone's language; (b) English for 1.x, translations later; (c) English only, by design |
| Round, Result sheet | "Clear the result" and "Reject" (a Lite phone's result) act on the first tap. Both are undone by entering the result again, but a stray tap on a full board costs a walk to the board. | (a) a confirm step for both; (b) undo in the notice ("Board 4: cleared. Undo") that re-enters what was there; (c) as now |
| Players, a player's sheet | "Withdraw from the tournament" acts on the first tap (red, but no question). Re-enter undoes it, unless a round is paired in between. | (a) a confirm that says the player is left out of the next pairing; (b) as now |
| More, Incidents | An incident cannot be edited or deleted from the phone, and a photo cannot be replaced or removed once sent. The API has no route for either - **changes the manubrium-v1 contract, so not touched.** | (a) add edit/delete (and photo replace) to the API and the app; (b) the desk only; (c) as now: incidents are a log |
| Round | Hand edits (swap, vacate, bye, fill, pool pair) have no undo of their own; the way back is the opposite edit, which the arbiter has to work out. | (a) "Undo the last edit" backed by the server's hand-edit history (contract change); (b) as now, with the opposite edit |
| Servers | Removing a server always signs it out; there is no "forget on this phone only". | (a) offer both; (b) as now (signing out is the safe default) |
